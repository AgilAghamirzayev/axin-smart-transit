SMART TRANSIT, EXPLAINED
Meet Axin: smarter bus journeys in Baku, with useful insights for transport operators.

01 / SET YOUR JOURNEY
First, search your destination. Use your location with permission, or choose another starting point. Axin checks nearby boarding stops, not just the closest.

02 / COMPARE OPTIONS
Compare up to three options, prioritizing fewer transfers. Tags highlight speed, crowding, and fewer boardings. Trips under one kilometre become walking routes.

03 / FOLLOW THE ROUTE
The map highlights your journey, boarding stops, transfers, and destination. Switch layers, zoom, or find your location. Simulated buses follow route geometry.

04 / UNDERSTAND ARRIVAL
Arrival time combines walking, waiting, riding, and transfers. The simulated fleet targets six-minute intervals. Times are estimates, without live traffic.

05 / SHARE CROWDING
Report low, medium, or high crowding. Reports are saved. Optional GPS participation requires consent and estimates participating devices, not every passenger.

06 / MONITOR THE NETWORK
Operators monitor routes, fleet activity, and crowding. Ten minutes of qualifying high crowding triggers an alert recommending an additional bus.

07 / ASSIGN A RESERVE BUS
Select a line, complete trajectory, and eligible reserve bus. The server validates and records the assignment. The bus starts in simulation; no physical bus is dispatched.

BUILT FOR TRANSPARENCY
